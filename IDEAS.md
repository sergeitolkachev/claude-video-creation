## Operator backlog

- **Inverse square.** Dose from a point source falls as 1/r², so three
  readings at three known points triangulate it with a dosimeter and a tape.
  Background in the cargo bay is up; ground blames a solar event, but a flare
  raises the whole volume evenly and his compartments differ sixfold. He
  solves the system on paper: the source sits, to half a metre, inside a
  container the manifest lists as empty.
- **Manifest.** F = ma: thrust is known to about a percent, the accelerometer
  gives a, so the ship can be weighed in flight. After one burn the dry mass
  is +312 kg; he logs it as error. After the next, +489 kg. Mass grows between
  burns and the airlock has not cycled. He computes how many days until the
  growth eats the return Δv. The record stops on that number.
- **Area.** Drag in low orbit scales with cross-section, so the decay rate
  converts directly into the station's area. Solar activity normal,
  atmospheric density normal; area is up 6.3 m². No panel deployed, no shield
  torn loose, the hull inspection is clean. Plotted over time, the area grew
  in steps, not smoothly.

## Documentary backlog

- **Martian perchlorate brines.** Real: calcium perchlorate brine has a
  eutectic near 199 K (−74 °C); perchlorate-respiring bacteria exist on Earth.
  Invented: a subsurface organism breathing perchlorate whose "season" is not
  a year but a temperature window a few hours long.
- **The Carboniferous oxygen ceiling.** Real: insect size is limited by
  diffusion through the tracheae; Palaeozoic giants (Meganeura, ~70 cm span)
  are tied to 30–35 % atmospheric oxygen and vanished when it fell. Invented:
  a lineage that evolved active ventilation instead of diffusion and survived
  the collapse. Told as a palaeontological investigation from impressions.
