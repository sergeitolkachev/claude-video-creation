**Scenes 3 and 5 are longer than this script planned, and no line was cut.**

## Synopsis

There is a depth below which the ocean contains no fish, and it is the same
depth everywhere. It is not set by pressure — crustaceans live two and a half
kilometres deeper. It is set by the chemistry of the molecule fish use to keep
their proteins working under load: the deeper the fish, the more of it they must
carry, and at about 8,200 m the concentration inside a fish meets the
concentration of the sea outside it.

The record then presents a specimen from 9,450 m that carries the same
protection bound into a polymer — full protection, almost no osmotic cost. The
polymer is held together by pressure. It comes apart at about 300 bar, which is
3,000 m below the ship, and releases its entire load into tissue built for a
fifth of it.

No specimen has ever reached the surface. The file closes on two numbers on two
cards: the deepest specimen confirmed, and the depth rating of the lander that
confirmed it.

## Style spine

Deep ocean far below the photic zone. The only light is hard artificial white
from one source, and it falls off to absolute black within a few metres;
nothing the source does not reach is lit. Fine pale marine snow drifts in
suspension throughout the water. Sediment is soft grey-brown silt with no
polish anywhere. Animal tissue is unpigmented, translucent off-white. The
palette is monochrome throughout: black water, cold white light, grey-brown
silt, off-white tissue. No colour, no warm light, no lettering, no markings,
no people.

**One set.** This record has no interiors and never reaches the surface. There
is no deck, no ship, no cabin and no dry shot anywhere in it — the ascent in
scene 6 is described in the voice and shown as open water. Nothing here needs a
second spine.

## Voice direction

Narrator. Unhurried, third person, present tense. A survey being read back, not
presented.

- The register does not change at scene 5, where the record stops describing
  published work and starts describing something that does not exist. Same pace,
  same weight, same flatness. If the delivery marks the transition, the record
  is broken.
- "Not fewer. None." is two flat statements, not a build. No emphasis on
  "None".
- "It is, osmotically, a shallow-water fish" is the flattest line in the
  episode. Read it like a units conversion.
- Numbers are read at the same level as everything else. No rising inflection
  on 9,450.
- The narrator never explains why any of this matters and never refers to the
  reader. There is no closing thought.

## Sound design

- Room tone under the entire record: low broadband water noise, no tonal
  content, never absolute silence.
- **Recurring element — the lander's pump.** A slow dull mechanical pulse,
  roughly one every four seconds, very low in the mix.
  - **Established** in scene 1, present through scenes 2 and 3.
  - **Removed** at the start of scene 4 and absent for the whole scene, under
    the empty plain and the prediction card. Nothing replaces it; the room tone
    thins.
  - **Returned** at the first frame of scene 5, at the same level as scene 1,
    under the descent past the line.
  - Runs through scene 6 and stops on the cut into the coda. Scene 7 is room
    tone alone.
- Shot 6.1 carries room tone and pump only. No stinger, no drop-out, no
  movement in the mix when the animal enters or leaves the beam.
- Video is generated with audio off throughout. Nothing above is prompted.

## Appendix — what is real and what is invented

### Real, verifiable

- **The hadal zone and the pressure gradient.** Seawater pressure rises by
  slightly over 1 bar per 10 m of depth. At 8,200 m that is about 830 bar; at
  10,935 m, about 1,100 bar. The Challenger Deep is approximately 10,935 m.
- **Pressure is not what stops fish.** Amphipod crustaceans, including
  *Hirondellea gigas*, are documented living and feeding at full Challenger Deep
  depth — roughly 2,700 m deeper than the deepest fish. The depth limit in this
  record applies to vertebrates only. This is the load-bearing fact of the
  episode and it is true.
- **TMAO and the measured concentrations.** Yancey, Gerringer, Drazen, Rowden
  and Jamieson, *PNAS* 2014, "Marine fish may be biochemically constrained from
  inhabiting the deepest ocean depths." Measured in the hadal snailfish
  *Notoliparis kermadecensis* from about 7,000 m in the Kermadec Trench:
  TMAO 386 ± 18 mmol/kg, internal osmolality 991 ± 22 mOsm/kg. Seawater is about
  1,100 mOsm/kg. Shallow marine teleosts run near 350 mOsm/kg.
- **The arithmetic of the limit.** Internal osmolality rises with depth as TMAO
  accumulates; seawater osmolality does not. Extrapolating the internal line to
  where it meets 1,100 mOsm/kg gives a maximum depth of approximately 8,200 m,
  with the paper and subsequent commentary putting the physiological ceiling in
  the 8,200–8,400 m band. Below it a fish carrying enough TMAO to keep its
  proteins working would be isosmotic with the sea.
- **Both depth records.** Deepest fish filmed: a *Pseudoliparis* snailfish in
  the Izu-Ogasawara Trench at 8,336 m, recorded August–September 2022 and
  published in 2023 — 136 m past the 8,200 m figure and inside the predicted
  band. Deepest fish caught: two *Pseudoliparis belyaevi* from the Japan Trench
  at 8,022 m. Nothing has been recorded below 8,336 m.
- **Osmotic pressure counts particles, not mass.** Colligative properties depend
  on the number of dissolved particles. Binding many units into one polymer
  genuinely reduces osmotic activity per unit of mass carried. The escape route
  this record proposes is chemically coherent even though the animal is not.

### Invented

- The polymerised protectant: its existence, its chain length, and its 300 bar
  stability threshold (≈ 3,000 m).
- Every figure attached to the specimen: depth 9,450 m, protection equivalent
  1,900 mmol/kg, osmotic cost 3 mOsm/kg, internal osmolality 380 mOsm/kg.
- The specimen itself, and the claim that six trenches have been surveyed below
  the line.
- The lander depth rating of 9,600 m, which exists only so that the last two
  cards can sit next to each other.

### Internal consistency

- **380 and 1,900 are not independent.** 1,900 ÷ 380 = 5 exactly, which is what
  makes "into tissue rated for three hundred and eighty" checkable rather than
  rhetorical. If either figure changes, the other must change with it.
- **380 mOsm/kg is deliberately inside the normal shallow marine teleost
  range** (≈ 350 mOsm/kg), not below it. The whole point of the invention is
  that the animal is unremarkable by every measure except depth. If this figure
  moves, the line "It is, osmotically, a shallow-water fish" goes with it.
- **The chain failure depth follows from the pressure gradient**, not from a
  chosen dramatic number: 300 bar ÷ 1.01 bar per 10 m ≈ 2,970 m, stated as
  3,000 m.
- **9,450 m implies about 950 bar**, roughly 15% more load than the 830 bar at
  the real limit. This is never spoken, but it is consistent: the record does not
  claim the animal solved pressure, only osmolality.
- **The Kermadec Trench is used twice on purpose.** The real measured snailfish
  and the invented specimen come from the same trench, so scene 5 reads as the
  same survey going deeper rather than as a new location.
