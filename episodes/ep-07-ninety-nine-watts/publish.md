FOR THOSE CHECKING THE ARITHMETIC

In vacuum, heat leaves only by radiation: P = εσAT⁴.
Radiator: 14.0 m², emissivity 0.85, plate at 285.5 K → 4,483 W.
Cross-check without trusting the coating: 0.22 kg/s × 3,400 J/(kg·K) × 6.0 K → 4,488 W. The two methods agree within 5 W.
Body heat: 2,150 kcal/day × 4,184 J/kcal ÷ 86,400 s → 104 W.
Balance: 4,483 − 4,280 − 104 = 99 W.
Radiator sensitivity: dP/dT = 4P/T ≈ 61.8 W/K. Rejecting 99 W more needs the plate 99 ÷ 61.8 = 1.60 K warmer — 285.5 K against a design 283.9 K.
Any refrigerator releases more heat into its room than it removes from its cold side.

Invented: the station, the radiator's size and coating, the bus load, the coolant loop, the operator and his diet, the forward hold.

**Notes for upload**

- The disclaimer is the first line, so it is what shows in the ~40 characters of the collapsed description on mobile.
- Chapters: YouTube needs the first one at 0:00, at least three, each at least 10 s long. All seven pass. The last chapter has no subtitle on purpose — giving it one names the refusal.
- The arithmetic block sits below the chapters, out of the collapsed preview. Its last physics line is the record's payoff stated as a general law, without connecting it to the patch. Viewers who connect it themselves are the comment section.
- The 6.0 K figure is the one the operator speaks ("six degrees"); at that rounding the calorimetric figure is 4,488 W, not 4,483. The description says "within 5 W" so nobody catches us claiming an exact match.
- Mark **Altered or synthetic content** at upload — this is required per video and cannot be set at channel level.
